Oxbow Meadows and the **Chattahoochee Valley Beekeepers Association** are teaming up for **Honey Harvest** — a public event where visitors can watch honey being extracted and bottled right on site!

## Event Details

- **When:** Saturday, July 11, 2026 — 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- **Where:** Oxbow Meadows, 3535 S Lumpkin Rd, Columbus, GA 31903

## Activities

- Extraction Demonstrations
- Honey Sales (starting at 12:00 PM, or as honey is extracted and bottled on site)
- And more!

## For CVBA Members

Members are encouraged to come out and **help with the harvest itself** — a great hands-on opportunity to be part of the process! **Tickets are not required for members working the event** — only the general public needs to purchase admission.

## Tickets & Extraction Show Times

Admission to a honey extraction demonstration is included with your admission ticket. At checkout, select a show time to attend. Please have a printed or electronic copy of your ticket for entry.
