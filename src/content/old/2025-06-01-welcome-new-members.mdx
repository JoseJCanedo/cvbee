The CVBA is growing! We welcomed **11 new members** at our May meeting — welcome to the hive, everyone.

## Getting Started

If you're brand new to beekeeping, here are the best ways to plug in:

- **Attend monthly meetings** — third Thursday, 6:30 PM
- **Join our mentorship program** — experienced members are paired with beginners for hands-on help in the bee yard
- **Check the updates page** — we post meeting recaps, educational content, and local beekeeping news here

## Mentorship Program

The CVBA mentorship program pairs new beekeepers with experienced mentors for the first full season. If you're interested, reach out to our Education Chair at the next meeting or via email.

## Resources

We recommend new beekeepers start with:

- *The Beekeeper's Handbook* by Diana Sammataro — excellent reference
- Georgia Beekeepers Association ([gabees.com](https://gabees.com)) — state-level resources and registration
- Local extension office — free soil and plant resources that pair well with beekeeping

Looking forward to learning and growing together this season!
