Spring is in full swing across the Chattahoochee Valley, and our members have been busy in the bee yard. Here's a roundup of what we've been seeing.

## Inspection Highlights

Most colonies came through winter in good shape this year. We're seeing strong populations and plenty of brood in most hives. A few things to keep an eye on:

- **Varroa mite loads** — do an alcohol wash or sugar roll before adding honey supers
- **Queen health** — look for a consistent laying pattern; spotty brood may indicate a queen issue
- **Swarm cells** — with populations booming, swarm prep is underway

## Swarm Season Is Here

If you find swarm cells, you have options: do a split, remove the cells (buying a few weeks), or let nature run its course and catch the swarm.

CVBA maintains a **swarm list** — if you're willing to catch local swarms, contact the club secretary to be added.
