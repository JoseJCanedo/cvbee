## What We Covered

Member Carlos walked us through a full extraction workflow:

1. Uncapping frames with a heated knife and cold scratcher
2. Loading a radial extractor
3. Straining and settling honey
4. **Checking moisture content** with a refractometer before bottling

The magic number is **18.6% or below**. Above that, honey can ferment in the jar.

## Partially Capped Frames

A common question came up: *can I extract from partially capped frames?*

The short answer — check the moisture first. If your refractometer reads under 18.6%, you're good to go even if the bees haven't finished capping. If it's higher, give the frames more time or use a dehumidifier in your extraction space overnight.
